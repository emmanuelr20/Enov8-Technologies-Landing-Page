## Stack

Existing Next.js application using React, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI primitives, Lucide/React Icons, and ScrollReveal. The repository includes a local development command (`pnpm dev`) and is configured for deployment on Vercel, Netlify, AWS Amplify, or comparable hosting.

## Users

Primary audiences are organizations and technology decision-makers in fintech, healthcare, education, logistics, enterprise, and e-commerce contexts. They come to the site to understand whether Enov8 can help them plan, modernize, secure, connect, automate, or deploy technology across their operations.

## Product Purpose

Enov8 Technologies is a Nigerian-based technology company that helps organizations turn complex technology challenges into practical digital solutions and competitive advantages. The website explains the service portfolio, establishes trust, provides service-specific detail, and gives prospective clients clear ways to start a consultation or submit an inquiry.

## Positioning

Enov8 combines strategic IT consulting with hands-on delivery across software, automation, identity and onboarding, security, enterprise systems, AI, networking, document workflows, digital signage, and hardware. Its distinct position is the ability to connect business transformation strategy to the technical implementation and operational support required to make that transformation work in practice.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically evaluate Enov8 while exploring a new project, replacing or modernizing legacy operations, improving internal efficiency, strengthening security, deploying enterprise systems, or looking for a long-term technology partner. The site supports this evaluation through the home page, about page, service index, individual service pages, downloadable brochure, contact form, direct email, phone/WhatsApp contact, and consultation booking flow.

## Product Principles
